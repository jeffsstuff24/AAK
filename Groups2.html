Which fruit do you want to learn about?

Kiwis Limes Pears

Kiwis

Kiwis (the fruit) are native to China and were originally called Chinese gooseberries. They grow on vines. 98% of kiwis grown in the US are grown in California.

Limes

Limes are a citrus fruit. They are valued for the acidity of their juice and are often used in Mexican, Vietnamese, and Thai dishes. Limes are also often used in cocktails.

Pears

Pears are pear-shaped. They should not operate heavy machinery, as they are a fruit and not sentient and do not have arms. Also, apparently pear flavored coffee is a thing.
